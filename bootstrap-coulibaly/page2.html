About Me

I am originally from Burkina Faso and moved to the United States to continue my education. Learning and adapting to a new environment has been an important part of my journey and has encouraged me to continue challenging myself academically and personally.

I enjoy being involved on campus and connecting with other students who are interested in technology. Being part of student organizations has given me opportunities to work with others, share ideas, and help create activities that support students interested in computer science and technology.

My goal is to continue learning and improving my skills in technology and cybersecurity. I am excited about the opportunities that lie ahead and look forward to building a career in this field.
